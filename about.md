Hi, I'm **Sinan Grehan** — a graduate student at the University of Chicago in the [Master's in Computational Analysis and Public Policy (MSCAPP)](https://harris.uchicago.edu/academics/programs-degrees/ms/ms-computational-analysis-and-public-policy) program.

I'm passionate about **civic tech**: using software and data to make government more effective and responsive to the people it serves. My work sits at the intersection of policy analysis and software development — whether that's building informative and useful programs for public use, or conducting econometric analyses to answer meaningful policy questions.

## What I'm working on

Right now I'm focused on **Data Centers Next Door**, a project exploring data centers in the Chicago metro area and their cost of living implications. More to come soon.
